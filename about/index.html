About Me

I'm a writing hobbyist, worldbuilding enthusiast, and programmer-hobbyist fascinated by fantasy, science fiction, mythology, and constructed languages. My writing combines expansive worldbuilding with a personal, often sarcastic voice, and I enjoy building fictional worlds from the ground up, from their histories and cultures to their languages, geography, and mythology. My current flagship series, The House That Held a God, follows Larnes Allricht, a god of chaos caught in conflicts far larger than himself, exploring magic, cosmic forces, ancient rivalries, and the occasional absurdity of existence. Outside writing, I'm interested in programming languages, compilers, interpreters, operating systems, virtual machines, and computer architecture. In both writing and programming, I like understanding how things work, then inevitably making them more complicated by building them myself. 😅😂

I also enjoy reading, watching movies and TV shows, and I also often create fanfics (not published, just stored on my laptop) about my favourite shows, books, movies, and worlds. My reading list includes Tolkien, George R.R. Martin, Dennis E. Taylor, Andy Weir, and many others. Douglas Adams is a particular favourite for being the god of comedic satire. I mostly read on Audible & Kindle, and I enjoy listening to podcasts such as Empire, and Let's Talk about Myths, Baby! on Amazon Music. I am a great fan & long-time listener of many classical songs. In fact, I have three playlists on Amazon Music: one for pop songs, one for rock songs, and one for frequent listens of mine. Some of my favourites are the Harry Potter track, the Lord of the Rings track, the Game of Thrones track, and the Star Wars track. Anytime I go on my Audible, I almost always am re-reading through the Bobiverse courtesy of Dennis E. Taylor, or I'm listening to Ready Player One, by Ernest Cline. I also enjoy Andy Weir's three books. If I go into any more detail about my reading list, or favourite songs, this website will become a book in itself. So, I will stop here. 😅
P.S. I'm also a great fan of the Greek & Norse Myths. That's all.
