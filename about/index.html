About · 01

Who I Am

My name is Aegon Targaryen. I am an aspiring author who writes serial fiction on Substack. I am an avid reader, a pretty good author (in my opinion), and a programmer hobbyist. I love a good world-building project, and I literally digest books. I like writing serials as opposed to standalone pieces. I like programming, and I am a very active participant of the holy text editor wars.

About · 02

Some of my hobbies

Well, I like writing (duh!) and I enjoy the feel of creating something. It's really important to me. I mainly write for the feel of creating. I like to make things. Preferrably, a lot of things, and interesting things. No promises, though. I also like to code, and to world-build for fun. In coding, I cover a lot of things - from programming languages, shells, interpreters, compilers, and my most recent project: "py2c".

What I Read (Books)

I like reading heavy, ponderous works, but at the same time, I don't mind casual, sarcastic, cynical works either. My reading list includes Ready Player One (and Two), by Ernest Cline, The Bobiverse, by Dennis E. Taylor, Andy Weir's books, and that's just a snapshot of my SF reading list. My fantasy list is longer: Harry Potter, JK Rowling, A Song of Ice and Fire, George RR Martin, The Lord of The Rings, JRR Tolkien, How to Train Your Dragon, Cressida Cowell, Percy Jackson, Rick Riordan, and Magnus Chase, Rick Riordan.

What I Read (Substack)

I have a truly enormous reading list on Substack. I like reading Kenneth Candler's works, with his Courier of Justice. I like Labyrinthia Mythweaver's work, with Basil the Bookshop Cat being my favourite. I read steffie's serial Terra and Tide regularly. I like J.A. Davidson's serial Blood and Iron. I also love J.E. Vance's work (and yes, I will stubbornly use his preferred pen name). I have a huge reading list apart from all these wonderful authors, include Posy Churchgate, OctiHaven, Hallie Jules, Bradley Ramsey, and many many more!

What I Like to Write

I really like writing fantasy serials, though I am capable of writing SF too. On my current Substack, I have not posted many of them, but it's a start. I like building intricate worlds and plots, and writing them. I really don't appreciate the next step, which is cutting out most of that detail under the guise of "editing". But, I digress.
