The author

Aegon Targaryen

Writer, programmer, worldbuilder, and professional overcomplicator of perfectly innocent ideas.

This is the personal site behind Words Forged in Fire, the idea of a publication for fiction, essays, notes, and assorted intellectual nonsense.

My interests sit somewhere between stories and systems: writing fictional worlds, designing programming languages, building compilers, exploring operating systems, and asking what happens when an idea is taken several steps further than it reasonably should be.

The publication lives on SubStack. This site acts as the wider archive and home for projects that do not fit neatly inside a publication feed.
